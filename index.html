How to play
  • New here? Play Boot Camp on the Campaign tab, or start a skirmish on Recruit with a Ceasefire. The Field guide in the top-left corner shows the next step.
  • Start: select your MCV and press D (or click it again) to deploy your Command Center.
  • Build: pick a structure from the sidebar. When it says READY, click it again and place it near your base.
  • Economy: Ore Refineries come with a Harvester. Gold ore and cyan lithium crystals are credits.
  • Power: keep the power bar green. Low power slows production, kills your minimap and shuts down defenses.
  • Touch: tap a unit to select it and tap the map to send it (tap an enemy to attack). Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, hold a finger still and then drag to box-select, double-tap a unit for all of its type. In the build bar tap to build, tap READY then tap the map and tap the outline again to place, hold a queued item to put it on hold or cancel. The buttons bottom-left stand in for the keyboard: clear, attack-move, stop, deploy, whole army, make a group (hold a group button to reassign it), jump to base and to the latest alert. Install it from your browser's menu (Add to Home Screen) for full screen.
  • Units: left-click/drag to select, right-click to move/attack. Ctrl+right-click or A = attack-move.
  • S stop · Ctrl+1-9 make group · 1-9 recall (twice to jump) · double-click selects all of a type (Ctrl for the whole map) · . next idle unit.
  • B G C V J K pick a build tab · the letter on each build button builds it · Z sell · X repair · F superweapon.
  • H jump to base · Space jump to the latest alert · click or drag the minimap to look around, right-click it to send units · P pause · F1 every key (or the Controls button).
  • Engineers capture enemy buildings. Heavy tanks crush infantry. Units promote to Veteran and Elite with kills.
  • Navy: build a Naval Shipyard out on the water near your base to launch patrol boats, destroyers, submarines and hovercraft (the Allies add a Missile Cruiser, the Axis a Battlecruiser, the UK an aircraft carrier and Japan an Aegis destroyer). Submarines are hidden while submerged and only destroyers and other submarines can hit them. Right-click a hovercraft with troops to board, drive it onto a beach and press D to unload. Pick Inland sea or Islands under Water for a naval map.
  • Garrisons: right-click a civilian building or your own Battle Bunker with infantry to move in; they fire out of it. Select the building and press D to empty it. Flame weapons burn occupants out.
  • Hills and bridges: hills rise above the plain behind cliffs and can only be climbed by their ramps. Units and defenses up top see two tiles further, reach a tile further and take less damage from fire coming up from below. Bridges carry ground units over narrow water (ships pass underneath): Ctrl+right-click one to bring it down, taking anything on it into the water, and right-click a broken bridge with an Engineer to rebuild it.
  • Dig in: select riflemen and press D to dig in behind sandbags (tougher, longer range, can't move). Any move order gets them up again.
  • Players: fight up to three computer players, alone or with a computer ally. Allies share vision. The opponent you pick leads the enemy side; the others get random factions.
  • Campaign: Operation Northwind, five missions of Allies against Axis that show off every country, with a story and objectives. Commando Raven joins you: enemies can't spot her from a distance until she opens fire, she drops infantry at long range, and right-clicking an enemy structure with her plants C4. Lose her and the mission fails. Space jumps to the latest alert or reinforcement.
  • Defense mode: no enemy base. Waves march on you from the other corners of the map, with super tanks every fifth wave. Survive them all to win. Clearing a wave pays a bounty, and Send now calls the next wave early for extra credits.

How to play
  • Start: select your MCV and press D (or click it again) to deploy your Command Center.
  • Build: pick a structure from the sidebar. When it says READY, click it again and place it near your base.
  • Economy: Ore Refineries come with a Harvester. Gold ore and cyan lithium crystals are credits.
  • Power: keep the power bar green. Low power slows production, kills your minimap and shuts down defenses.
  • Units: left-click/drag to select, right-click to move/attack. Ctrl+right-click or A = attack-move.
  • S stop · Ctrl+1-9 make group · 1-9 recall (twice to jump) · double-click selects all of a type.
  • Tab cycles build tabs · Q…I build hotkeys · Z sell · X repair · F superweapon.
  • H jump to base · Space jump to last alert · P pause (and save or load) · F5 quicksave · F9 quickload · M mute · N next track · arrows / screen edges / middle-drag scroll.
  • Engineers capture enemy buildings. Heavy tanks crush infantry. Units promote to Veteran and Elite with kills.
  • Garrisons: right-click a civilian building or your own Battle Bunker with infantry to move in; they fire out of it. Select the building and press D to empty it. Flame weapons burn occupants out.
  • Dig in: select riflemen and press D to dig in behind sandbags (tougher, longer range, can't move). Any move order gets them up again.
  • Players: fight up to three computer players, alone or with a computer ally. Allies share vision. The opponent you pick leads the enemy side; the others get random factions.
  • Campaign: Operation Northwind, four missions with a story and objectives. Commando Raven joins you: enemies can't spot her from a distance until she opens fire, she drops infantry at long range, and right-clicking an enemy structure with her plants C4. Lose her and the mission fails. Space jumps to the latest alert or reinforcement.
  • Defense mode: no enemy base. Waves march on you from the other corners of the map, with super tanks every fifth wave. Survive them all to win. Clearing a wave pays a bounty, and Send now calls the next wave early for extra credits.

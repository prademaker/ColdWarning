How to play
  • Start: select your MCV and press D (or click it again) to deploy your Command Center.
  • Build: pick a structure from the sidebar. When it says READY, click it again and place it near your base.
  • Economy: Ore Refineries come with a Harvester. Gold ore and cyan lithium crystals are credits.
  • Power: keep the power bar green. Low power slows production, kills your minimap and shuts down defenses.
  • Units: left-click/drag to select, right-click to move/attack. Ctrl+right-click or A = attack-move.
  • S stop · Ctrl+1-9 make group · 1-9 recall (twice to jump) · double-click selects all of a type.
  • Tab cycles build tabs · Q…I build hotkeys · Z sell · X repair · F superweapon.
  • H jump to base · Space jump to last alert · P pause · M mute · arrows / screen edges / middle-drag scroll.
  • Engineers capture enemy buildings. Heavy tanks crush infantry. Units promote to Veteran and Elite with kills.
